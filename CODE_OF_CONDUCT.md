# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participating in pu-get to be a respectful, harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity,
sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste,
colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- showing empathy and kindness;
- respecting differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences;
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback;
- accepting responsibility, apologising to those affected by mistakes, and learning
  from the experience;
- focusing on what is best for the project and its users.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention;
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing someone else's private information without explicit permission;
- encouraging or normalising copyright infringement or the redistribution of paid
  material;
- other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
  setting.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews)
and when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards.
They may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other
contributions that do not align with this code, and will communicate moderation
reasons when appropriate.

Report concerns privately. Use GitHub's report-abuse flow, or contact the
maintainer through the GitHub profile linked from the repository. Do not post
personal details or a sensitive report in a public issue.

All reports will be reviewed as promptly as possible and handled with respect for
the privacy and safety of the reporter and everyone involved.

## Enforcement guidelines

Maintainers may use the following ladder depending on impact and repetition:

1. **Correction** — a private explanation of the problem and the expected behavior.
2. **Warning** — a warning with consequences for continued behavior, including a
   request to avoid interaction for a period.
3. **Temporary ban** — a temporary ban from interaction with the project.
4. **Permanent ban** — a permanent ban for a pattern of violations, harassment or
   aggression.
